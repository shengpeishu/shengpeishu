# Introduction

Five paragraphs is the ordinary length. Do not open paragraph 1 with "In this paper." About 160 words in a paragraph, four to eight sentences. The end of paragraph 5 is a one- or two-sentence roadmap, and that roadmap is the only one.

1. The setting, in the institution's own terms. A standard, a filing rule, a workload fact, or a press claim. One or two paragraphs if the rule needs the room. Not a scene, and not a statistic that is not later in the design.
2. The prior result, and why it is incomplete. "Prior research indicates that..." The next sentence bounds it: the missing moderator.
3. "In this paper, we..." Design preview, then the result preview. The result preview states the interaction. It may include the direction. It does not include an F-statistic.
4. Contribution as boundary conditions on one or two named streams. "In doing so, we extend..." Name the streams the assignment named. Do not add a literature.
5. Roadmap. Which section does what. Two sentences.

If the theory does not sign a cell, the preview says so, or it saves that cell for a labeled RQ. Do not import a finding from the five papers you read for cadence.
