# Method outline

Use this order. Short headings, one to six words. Say only what the assignment supplies. Where a fact is missing, write "[not supplied]" rather than a plausible pool, exclusion count, or stimulus sentence.

## Participants

Who makes this decision, their experience, and the exclusions. Give the count and the reason if both were supplied. Students are allowed when the assignment says students. Say what kind, and do not upgrade them to practitioners.

## Design

The factorial, in one sentence. Name the factors. A 2×2 is a design, not the contribution. A second experiment gets its own heading and says only what changed.

## Independent variables

One rewritten sentence of the manipulation, if the assignment gives the wording. State what is held constant. Do not tour an appendix. Do not paste a stimulus from the corpus.

## Dependent variables

The same noun as the hypothesis. Scale endpoints in parentheses. If the measure is an average of two questions, leave the correlation or alpha for the results.

## Procedure

The order the participant sees. A within-subjects factor gets a short subsection. Stop. The result does not belong in this outline.
