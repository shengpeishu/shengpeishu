# Cognitive Framework

> L3–L5 analysis of topic logic, source strategy, and cognitive frames. Evidence-supported patterns are separated from tentative inferences at the end of each section. This is a mixed-author corpus. The frames below are the ones that recur across papers with different bylines. They are not a biography.

## Topic Logic

### What starts a paper

The trigger is a conflict that a new study can split, not a news event. Three triggers recur.

1. A standard or a filing rule treats two mechanisms as complements, substitutes, or optional, and the psychology says the choice will change the judgment. Risk-factor disclosure that forbids a risk-management discussion, versus market-risk disclosure that requires one. Plain-English rules that tell managers to drop jargon. Materiality guidance that tries to make qualitative factors salient. Fair-value standards that are accused of changing real decisions. The paper does not take the regulator's side in the introduction. It takes the disagreement as the occasion for a design.
2. Prior papers disagree, and the disagreement can be restated as a missing moderator. Non-audit fees impair independence in some studies and not in others, until specialization is in the model. Guidance form does not change earnings estimates, until the investor has a long or short position. Accountability helps in some subject pools and not in others, until knowledge and task complexity are measured. The paper's promise is to say under which condition each side of the mix appears.
3. A press claim and a published result point in opposite directions. Analysts are said to punish firms that fail to warn; an archival paper finds that firms that warn are punished more. The experiment is built to separate those two stories (a belief about warners, versus the order in which the warning and the announcement are processed).

The timing is follow-and-bound, not first-mover commentary and not a postmortem. The paper arrives after a literature and a rule already exist. It does not announce a phenomenon. It says the phenomenon has been described too broadly.

### The angle

The angle is the decision maker's processing, under a feature the institution already contains. Not the firm's strategy as strategy, and not the reader's moral position. Auditors review a memo, book or waive a difference, rely on an analytic, or staff a busy season. Investors value a stock after a disclosure. Managers choose a hedge. Analysts revise a forecast. The narrator is outside the decision, writing "we examine," and the theory is about what that person attends to.

The preferred cut is a joint effect. A title that says "the joint effect of X and Y" is a content choice, not a decoration. Main effects are the setup. The sentence the paper is willing to be quoted on names the condition.

### What these papers do not take up

- A practice guide, a how-to, or a firm profile.
- An interview as the article. Interviews appear only to justify a measure.
- A single-firm scandal narrative.
- A claim that a judgment bias is a character flaw. The remedy, when there is one, is a change in the task: generate an alternative hypothesis, make an independent assessment first, raise the salience of the economic column, present net income from fair value in a separate column, prompt a growth mindset.
- Topics with no judgment in them. Even the archival papers are about a judgment or a quality outcome (opinion, staffing, realization rate), not about a pure capital-markets description.

### Evidence versus inference

Supported by the openings of the experimental and archival papers: the trigger is a bounded conflict with prior work or with a rule; the contribution sentence uses "conditional on," "moderated by," or "joint effect"; the implied reader of the last section is a standard setter, a regulator, an audit firm, or a manager.

Tentative: the corpus's center of gravity is judgment and decision making in accounting, so the absence of tax, governmental, and pure valuation papers may be a property of this sample rather than a rule these authors would state. Do not treat "they never write about tax" as a discovered prohibition beyond this set of 20.

## Source Strategy

### What is allowed to carry a claim

| Role | What is used | How it is used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The result | The study's own experiment, archive, or field evaluations | Reported with the design visible. A result without the comparison that produced it is not written. |
| The prediction | A named psychological mechanism | Attribution, impression formation, affect-as-information, motivated reasoning, memory for inconsistent evidence, mindset. One mechanism per interaction. Cited at the point the prediction is derived. |
| The occasion | A standard, a regulator's speech, or a handbook | Motivates the question. Does not supply the prediction. SAB No. 99, SFAC No. 8, Regulation S-K, the Plain English Handbook, PCAOB inspection remarks. |
| The conflict | One or two prior accounting papers | Quoted for their result, not for their status. "Kasznik and Lev found X" is the use. A prestige adjective is not. |
| The measure, in archival work | Several proxies of one construct | Each proxy is introduced with the error it is known to have. Discretionary accruals are described as noisier than opinions or restatements. The paper then shows the interaction on more than one proxy. |
| The participant | The person who makes this decision in the wild | Auditors from a firm for an audit task, with years of experience reported. Alumni or managers for an investor or a hedging task. Analysts for an analyst task. Students are used and then bounded ("third-year," "PMBA"), not hidden. |
| The anecdote | A press line, used once | To set up a conflict with a published result. It is not evidence for the hypothesis. |

### How disagreement and uncertainty are handled

Mixed evidence is the reason to add a factor, and the paper says so in the introduction. A null that the theory predicted is reported with the same grammar as a significant effect: the test, the means, the p-value, and the clause "as predicted" or "we find no difference." Unexpected nulls are not redescribed as "directional support."

Tails are declared. Directional predictions are one-tailed and marked. Everything else is two-tailed. The declaration is one sentence, often a footnote, and then the table note repeats it. p-values are given as numbers ("p = 0.018"), not as a star alone, though stars also appear in regression tables.

Sensitivity is a second specification, not an adverb. "Robust" is earned by naming the alternative cutoff or the alternative proxy and saying whether the coefficient kept its sign. In the experiment papers the parallel move is a second experiment that turns the proposed remedy on and off.

Limitations name the threat: one memo format, one country, one office, a proxy with low power, participants who may have assumed a negotiation the design did not control. The sentence after the threat is the study that would answer it.

### Figures as sources

Sampled across the jargon, fair-value, mindset, readability, CSR, internal-control, tone, risk-disclosure, workload, and older journal papers. Logos were ignored.

- A predicted-pattern figure is a source for the hypothesis. It shows the shape the theory allows, including a shaded region when the theory does not pin down a cell mean. It is not a result.
- A results figure repeats the table in a form the interaction can be seen in. It does not contain a number the table does not have.
- A mediation diagram is a source for the process claim. The coefficient, the p-value, and the indirect-effect interval are on the figure. The prose states the same path in a sentence.
- A process schematic in an archival paper (the workload paper's chevron of planning, interim work, and year-end hours) defines variables. It is a measurement exhibit.
- A stimulus table (hedge versus not-hedge earnings, a CSR scorecard) is the manipulation. The prose tells the reader which column changed. The table is the evidence that the economics were held constant.
- Full-page scans in the older JSTOR files are the article itself plus a journal logo. They are not a visual argument.

Precise cell means live in the table and are repeated in the prose. They do not live only in a figure. If a figure were deleted, the result would still be recoverable from the text. That is the division of labor.

### Evidence versus inference

Supported: psychology for the prediction, standards for the occasion, own data for the result, multiple proxies when the construct is audit quality, one-tailed tests only for directional predictions, remedies implemented as design changes.

Tentative: the preference for a 90 percent interval on an indirect effect is visible in the mediation figures that were sampled (jargon, and the notes that cite Hayes). It is the right way to report mediation when imitating those papers. It is not established as a rule of the archival papers, which do not use that design.

## Cognitive Frames

These are the propositions a new paper in this voice has to be able to live with. They are not slogans. Each one is a constraint on what may be claimed.

### 1. A main effect is incomplete until the condition is named

The contribution line across the experimental papers, and across the archival papers that engage a mixed literature, is that a previously reported effect appears, disappears, or reverses under a second feature. Industry knowledge changes what jargon does. News valence and position change what guidance form does. Readability changes judgments when benchmarks conflict, and changes them differently when benchmarks agree. Specialization changes the non-audit-fee association. A qualitative-materiality threshold changes whether salience changes the book-or-waive decision. Risk materialization turns off an effect that existed before the risk arrived. Write the finding as "when," not as "in general."

### 2. The person, the task, and the setting have to match

Accountability does not raise performance unless the person has the knowledge and the problem-solving ability the task demands. Technical knowledge separates staff and seniors; tacit managerial knowledge separates managers. A growth mindset changes reliance on an analytic when inspection risk is high, which is when the task is aversive. Specialists are not described as uniformly higher quality. The protection shows up against a threat (non-audit fees, a busy season, a complex task). A claim that "experience helps" or "accountability helps" with no match to the task is the claim this corpus writes against.

### 3. Attention is the path from information to the judgment

Holding the economics constant is the design, and the result is still allowed to move. A justification memo that repeats supporting evidence reduces the error hypotheses a reviewer generates, even though the workpapers contain the inconsistent evidence. Fair-value volatility on the page leads accountants to refuse a hedge that is economically sound, and a separate column or a more salient economic total reduces that refusal. Positive words move earnings judgments when readability and sophistication let them, and backfire when they do not. Salience of a qualitative factor changes a booking decision for auditors whose own threshold is low, until the client states a concern. The corpus treats the lever as presentation, order, and instruction. It does not treat the lever as telling the decision maker to be more professional.

### 4. Positive and negative cases are not mirrors

Negative CSR, negative news, bad jargon for a knowledgeable reader, and a materialized risk do not simply flip the positive result. Several papers' entire contribution is the asymmetry: the heuristic operates for immaterial positive CSR and not for negative CSR; guidance form matters for positive news and not for negative news; a positive tone is helpful before a risk materializes and is not helpful, or is costly, after. Do not write a symmetric interaction because the design was a 2×2. Sign only the cells the theory signs. Report the other cells as what they were.

### 5. A construct you cannot see cleanly is tested with more than one imperfect look

Audit quality, independence, and expertise are treated as not identical to any one proxy. The archival papers say so and then bring opinions, accruals, forecasts, and market response, or realization rates plus client-service scores plus audit adjustments. The field paper refuses laboratory task performance as a stand-in for the firm's own evaluations, and then still measures technical knowledge, problem-solving ability, and tacit managerial knowledge separately. One clean proxy is not the virtue here. A disclosed, noisy proxy, repeated, is.

### 6. The theory is psychological and the reader of the implication is institutional

The prediction is derived from a mechanism a psychologist would recognize. The last section's "should care" sentence is addressed to someone who writes a rule or runs an engagement: the SEC's choice to forbid or require a risk-management discussion, a standard setter's fair-value presentation, a firm's review process, a national office's staffing of the busy season. Do not end on the mechanism alone, and do not end on a regulatory recommendation the design did not test. The implication is what the result changes in that reader's decision.

### Evidence versus inference

Propositions 1 through 4 are supported by the hypothesis and conclusion language of multiple experiments that do not share a full byline (auditor-judgment papers, investor-judgment papers, the managerial hedging paper). Proposition 5 is supported by the three archival papers and the field paper. Proposition 6 is supported by the pairing, inside the same papers, of a psychology citation in the hypothesis section and a standards or firm implication in the close.

Tentative, and not a rule to force: the corpus often prefers an ordinal interaction and a contrast test over a claim that every cell differs from every other cell. That shows up in predicted figures that shade a region rather than plot a point, and in contrast weights written into the table note. A new paper should do this when the theory is ordinal. It should not do it when the theory really does sign every cell.

Also tentative: "configural" processing, substitutes versus complements, is the explicit frame of the internal-control paper and is a fair description of several interactions elsewhere. Use the word only when the claim is that two institutional mechanisms replace rather than add to each other. It is not a synonym for "interaction."
