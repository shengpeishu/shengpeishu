# Experiment abstract

120–220 words. One paragraph. Four moves, in this order. At most one citation, and only if the assignment supplied it. No roadmap. Keywords on the next line, constructs separated by semicolons, if the assignment asks for them.

1. What is manipulated or measured, and who decides. "We conduct [one or two] experiments to examine..."
2. The design in one clause, if the assignment gives the participants or the factors. Skip the clause if it does not.
3. The interaction. Name the condition: "when A, but not when B." Do not write the main effect as the finding. Do not mirror the cells the theory did not sign.
4. Who the result informs, or the boundary it places on a named stream. One sentence. A decision, not a slogan.

The mechanism, if the assignment states one, sits between the interaction and the boundary, in the assignment's own strength. Do not add a citation, a mean, or a participant pool.

Delete these instructions before the draft. Do not leave the numbers in the prose.
