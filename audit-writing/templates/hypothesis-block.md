# Hypothesis block

One subsection per construct, then a subsection for the interaction. Each subsection is four to eight sentences. The first states the causal claim. The middle sentences carry the citations the assignment supplied, and the boundary. The last says what that implies here. Then one short handoff is allowed.

A moderator the theory does not sign is an RQ, not a hypothesis. If the theory only signs an ordinal pattern, say the prediction figure depicts the possible patterns. Do not draw every cell as different. No p-value in the hypothesis.

Handoff, then the hypothesis in its own paragraph:

This discussion suggests the following hypothesis.

**Hypothesis 1.** When [moderator], [decision makers] [verb] [dependent variable] more than when [other level].

Use the assignment's nouns. Repeat the dependent variable. Do not replace it with "this outcome." Bold only the label. The hypothesis sentence is not bold.

**Hypothesis 2.** The difference in Hypothesis 1 is larger when [second moderator] than when [other level].

Delete any hypothesis the assignment did not sign.
